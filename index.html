As part of Oklahoma City’s proposed code update and parking development standards, non-residential uses will no longer have minimum parking requirements. Instead, Oklahoma City is proposing a new way to manage and regulate parking requirements for development, the Parking Threshold. The Parking Threshold acts as a soft cap on the number of spaces provided as off-street parking. Parking above the calculated threshold is still allowed, but requires additional landscaping requirements in contrast to parking areas operating at or below the calculated threshold provided.

Residential minimums depend on dwelling type, dwelling size, and street width. Dwellings in DD or MX zones are exempt.
